Overview

Labour's upward trajectory continues through October as Burnham has refreshed Labour's image since his elevation. However, these poll results may not reflect sentiment on the autumn budget statement as this was delivered late in the month. This set of polls appears to be the most similar comparison to the 2024 election yet. Labour now hold a plurality in the entirety of the North of England. In addition, there has been a large swing in Scotland's central belt to Labour from the SNP (though the SNP keep the plurality of Scottish seats). Compared to their height a year ago, Reform's support now only remains in larger numbers in coastal towns and urban peripheries. The Conservatives appear to have benefitted most from this swing, and rise to second place.

The shifts in poll numbers appear more stark in first-past-the-post, which strongly favours the winner. Proportional modelling more accurately reflects the gradual shift in support as well as the popular support of each party.
