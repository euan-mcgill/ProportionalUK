Overview

This month, there is already the green shoots of a "Burnham Bounce", with Labour recovering a lot of its heartlands from both Reform and the Greens. Both of these parties continue their decline in the polls after bad headlines for both their leaders Farage and Polanski. The Conservatives, too, continue their upward trend in the polls. There was an unusally bad showing for Plaid in Wales, possibly owing to a lack of immediate results from their victory in the Senedd in May.
